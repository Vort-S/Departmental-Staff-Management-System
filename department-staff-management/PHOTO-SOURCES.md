# Photography and licensing

The public-facing landing and account pages use three external editorial images from Unsplash (not staff portraits). They are displayed via Next.js Image with an approved `images.unsplash.com` remote pattern and AVIF/WebP optimization. If a CDN image fails, the UI displays an accessible icon and a neutral fallback rather than a broken image.

Licensing information: https://unsplash.com/license. Unsplash permits free use including commercial use. Check the source and current license before republishing these images under a different policy. Photos represent campus life, not this institution, its employees, or actual users of DSMS.

An official institution logo and institution-approved department photos would improve authenticity. No stock photograph is used to identify a member of staff.
